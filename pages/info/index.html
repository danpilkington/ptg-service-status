Service information

One place to understand operational service health.

The dashboard is designed to provide users and IT teams with a simple, consistent view of reported service availability. It separates externally managed Microsoft services from systems operated directly by Progressive Technology Group, making it easier to understand where an issue may originate.

01

Service health

Understand the current state.

Each monitored service is assigned a clear status based on the latest available operational information.

Operational

The service is operating normally with no known availability issues.

Degraded

The service remains available but performance or functionality may be reduced or intermittent.

Outage

The service is unavailable or experiencing significant disruption.

Maintenance

Planned technical work may temporarily affect service performance or availability.

02

Service ownership

Microsoft and PTG managed services.

Services are grouped according to the organisation responsible for operating the underlying platform.

M Microsoft-managed

Includes Microsoft cloud platforms and services whose availability is primarily determined by Microsoft's infrastructure and published service health information.

P PTG-managed

Includes infrastructure, applications and services operated or supported directly by Progressive Technology Group.

Important: Dashboard status is intended as operational guidance and does not replace local troubleshooting or an official advisory issued by the relevant service provider.
